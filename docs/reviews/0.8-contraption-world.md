# Contraption review world

This world is a separate developer instance. The starter next to it launches
the matching native build. It uses the original Create models when the private
6.0.10 JAR is present. The same blocks and recipes work with the built-in models.

| Station | Position | What to try |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanical Bearing | 904, 84, 56 | Flip the lever to stop and restart the rotor. Land on top in Survival and stand away from the center to check that it carries you. |
| Mounted chest | 904, 84, 62 | Stop the bearing with its lever. Open the chest, add an item, restart and stop the bearing again. Check both the item and the facing of the chest. |
| Sideways Bearing | 916, 85, 55 | Watch the logs rotate around a horizontal axis. |
| Windmill Bearing | 904, 84, 72 | Use an empty hand to stop and restart it. The colored sails return to their starting positions. The rear shaft runs at 1 RPM. The orange sail outside the rotation plane stays put. With the windmill stopped, try dyeing a sail or using shears to turn it into a frame. |
| Mechanical Piston | 919, 83, 68 | Use the lever to reverse travel. The normal head pushes the plank and leaves it at the far end. |
| Sticky Mechanical Piston | 919, 83, 75 | Use the lever to see the sticky head bring the plank back. |
| Clockwork Bearing | 919, 85, 70 | Watch both hands; use an empty hand to stop and start them. The two logs should return to their original positions together. |
| Harvester | 904, 82, 88 | Release the clutch. Ripe crops are harvested and replanted. The half-grown wheat stays untouched. Stop the bearing and inspect the chest for the harvest. |
| Moving Saw | 919, 82, 88 | Release the clutch. The saw should pause at the tree, cut the trunk and leaves, then resume. The separate log stays in place. |
| Rope Pulley | 904, 85, 103 | Use the right lever to release the clutch. The magnet travels down and catches the log. Use the left lever to reverse it. |
| Gantry | 918, 83, 103 | Use the right lever to release the rail stop. The carriage takes the plank down the rail. Use the left lever to reverse it. |
| Stationary Saw | 919, 82, 110 | Release the clutch. The saw cuts the trunk and canopy beside it. |
| Mechanical Plough | 904, 82, 120 | Release the clutch. Grass becomes farmland, coarse and rooted dirt become dirt, and the plough clears three snow layers. The wheat stays planted. |
| Tree farm | 920, 82, 120 | Release the clutch and let the rotor make a few turns. The Saw cuts the three-block trunk. The Deployer plants seven new saplings from its hand and the chest. Stop the bearing and check for seven logs in the chest. |
| Craft a Deployer | 924, 81, 125 | Take materials from the chest, then craft Sand Paper and Rose Quartz. Hold use with the paper and quartz in opposite hands to polish it. Use the result for the Electron Tube, then craft the Brass Hand and Deployer. |
| Super Glue | 904, 82, 136 | With Super Glue in hand, right-click each plank once. Release the clutch. Both planks should rotate together. Stop the bearing and check that the glued planks land together. Attack through a glued volume while holding the glue to remove it. |
| Upward Saw | 920, 82, 136 | Drop one oak log onto the blade. Pick up the stripped log and drop it back onto the blade. The second pass yields six planks. |
| Linear Chassis | 904, 82, 152 | Release the clutch. The two planks within range 2 move with the chassis. The third plank and the nearby Secondary Chassis stay put. |
| Radial Chassis | 920, 82, 152 | Release the clutch. The diagonal plank inside the range moves with the arm. The far corner stays put. |

The fields and tree can be tested once without resetting their blocks. Random
ticks are disabled to keep crop ages and saplings steady in this world. Take a
world backup or copy before the destructive stations if you want to replay
them from the beginning.

Try saving and reopening the world while a rotor is active. For a separate
recovery test, place a block where a rotor would land and remove the bearing.
Clear the obstruction and check that the payload returns once. This adapter
requires the whole swept volume to fit within one loaded chunk column. Moving
chest menus, multiple inventories, chassis on other drivers, glue previews,
passenger edge cases, exact presentation and Sable physics remain open.
